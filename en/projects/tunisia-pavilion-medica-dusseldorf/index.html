The Tunisia pavilion at MEDICA 2026, Düsseldorf.

A national pavilion at the largest medical trade fair: several Tunisian companies, each with its own stand, brought together under one identity. The sign you see from afar is the suspended light ring branded “Tunisia — Your health… Yes We Care”.

Get a quote for a similar set-upThe pavilion, stand by stand

In short. For MEDICA 2026 (Messe Düsseldorf, 16-19 November 2026) Danova Stand built the Tunisia national pavilion: an area open on several sides and divided into stands for individual medical companies, including Bioservice Tunisie, PEC MI, Medika Hitex and Alberton Medical, plus the Tunisia Export lounge. Above it, a suspended ring in backlit fabric carrying the country brand; on the floor, a platform with wood-effect flooring and an LED edge; for each company a red column with its name, white walls, a backlit graphic, lit shelves and display cases.

The pavilion, stand by stand

One pavilion, several companies

In a national pavilion every company wants its own space, yet visitors must see at a glance that they are in one country's pavilion. So we repeated the same layout in every stand: a red column with a medical icon pattern and the company name, white walls and a backlit graphic with the exhibitor's content.

Inside each stand only what the company needs changes: chrome rails to hang products, lit shelves in the niches, display towers or counters with brochure holders, a table and seating for meetings and, where needed, a lockable storeroom for stock and bags.

The light ring above the pavilion

MEDICA has thousands of exhibitors, so the pavilion needed a sign visible from afar. The suspended ring, in backlit fabric, carries the “Tunisia — Your health… Yes We Care” brand and the flag on several sides, so the pavilion stands out from the surrounding aisles.

On the floor, the platform with wood-effect flooring and an aluminium LED edge defines the space and ties the stands together. For a fair abroad everything is prepared before departure: graphics, furniture and lighting arrive ready to install.

Yes. At MEDICA 2026 (16-19 November) we built the Tunisia national pavilion, with a stand for each medical company and the Tunisia Export lounge. We handle design, production, transport and installation in Germany too.

Do you build national pavilions with several exhibitors?

Yes: we set a common layout (country identity, suspended sign, platform, colours) and adapt it to each company with graphics, display cases and product display. Each exhibitor has its own stand; visitors see one pavilion.
