What IFAT Munich is and what it asks of a stand

IFAT Munich is the world's leading trade fair for water, sewage, waste and raw materials management. It takes place every two years in May at Messe München, with halls and outdoor areas for vehicles and machines.

Exhibitors bring compactors, shredders, collection vehicles, pumps and treatment plants. You need load-bearing platforms, equipped outdoor areas and spaces to explain complex processes.

What a stand for IFAT Munich needs

  • Load-bearing platforms. For collection and treatment machines and vehicles, with calculated loads and ramps for vehicle entry.
  • Outdoor areas. Offices and roofs for open-air demos, with surfaces suited to heavy vehicles and May weather.
  • Demo plants. Water and power where needed, with closed circuits and floor protection to show real operation.
  • Explaining the process. Screens, models and technical graphics telling the water or waste cycle, from collection to recovery.
  • Meeting rooms. For utilities, designers and operators, where tenders, plants and supply are discussed.

What your stand builder does at IFAT Munich

For IFAT Munich we handle design and 3D renders, production, graphics, electrics, transport, build and dismantling at Messe München, or just the part you are missing, even only the build of a stand you already own. In particular:

  • Stands for IFAT.
  • Areas for vehicles and machines.
  • Demo plants.
  • Build at Messe München.

From Italy to Munich with one contact person

Your IFAT Munich stand is produced in Italy by our partner workshops, checked in our warehouse and travels with us to Messe München. There it is built by crews coordinated by one project manager, who also handles passes, unloading slots and paperwork with the organiser. After the fair, if the stand is yours, it goes back to our warehouse for the next edition or for another fair.

When to start preparing your IFAT Munich stand

IFAT takes place in even years: the next is expected in May 2028. For outdoor areas and demo plants it is best to start at least nine months ahead, because connections and permits take time. We update the official dates as soon as they are out.

All dates of upcoming trade fairs in Italy and Europe are in our trade fair calendar 2026–2027 (in Italian).
