Our service
We build stands for Ipack-Ima and for the trade fairs of the sector in Italy and across Europe: design, production, transport and build.

What Ipack-Ima is and what it asks of a stand

Ipack-Ima is the international fair of processing and packaging technology: packaging machines, complete lines, materials, automation and solutions for food, pharma, cosmetics and consumer goods. It takes place at Fiera Milano Rho and brings manufacturers and customers from all over the world.

Machines come to the fair running, often linked in a line: you need floors that carry heavy loads, power, compressed air, guards and room around for the public. Larger manufacturers choose double-decker stands, with meeting rooms above and machines below.

What a stand for Ipack-Ima needs

  • Platforms for working lines. Load-bearing floors with routes for cables and pipes, calculated loads.
  • Services. Power, compressed air and drains requested within the regulations' deadlines.
  • Guards and distances. Barriers and routes for safe demonstrations.
  • Double-decker stands. Meeting rooms upstairs without taking space from the machines.
  • Heavy handling. Lifting equipment and machine entry times coordinated.

What your stand builder does at Ipack-Ima

For Ipack-Ima we handle design and 3D renders, production, graphics, electrics, transport, build and dismantling at Fiera Milano Rho, or just the part you are missing, even only the build of a stand you already own. In particular:

  • Stands for Ipack-Ima.
  • Platforms for packaging lines.
  • Double-decker stands.
  • Build at Fiera Milano Rho.

When to start preparing your Ipack-Ima stand

The next Ipack-Ima is expected in 2028. For stands with working lines or a double-decker, start at least a year before, together with the choice of machines. We update the official dates as soon as they are out.
