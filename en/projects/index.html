Our projects: real photos, not renders.

This is where we collect finished jobs, with photos taken on site by our crew: the brief, what we built, how long it took and what the client said.

Danova Stand publishes its completed projects here with real set-up photos: event, show, trade fair and exhibition set-ups in Italy and Europe. Each case study lists the venue, the dates, the work carried out and, where the client agrees, their feedback.

Each case study describes a real job: where and when, what the client asked for, what we built and what other suppliers did, the problems we met on site and how we solved them. The photos are the ones our crew took during the set-up, not renders or catalogue images.

We work at trade fairs, at event venues and in places that are hard to reach, like Venice, where everything arrives by boat. If you need a similar set-up, tell us where and when: we reply in writing with an itemised quote within 24 working hours.
