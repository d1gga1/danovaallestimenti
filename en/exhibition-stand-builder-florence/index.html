Stands in Florence, from design to installation

Where we work: Fortezza da Basso and Florence congress centres.

Florence hosts fairs and congresses in venues unlike any other in Italy: the Fortezza da Basso, a sixteenth-century fortress turned exhibition centre, the Palazzo dei Congressi and the Palazzo degli Affari. It is the home of Pitti Immagine, with Pitti Uomo, Pitti Bimbo, Pitti Filati and Taste.

Building in a historic venue means precise rules on fixings, loads, heights and vehicle access. Stands must be free-standing, light and quick to build, but finished like a boutique.

What to know about building a stand in Florence

  • Historic venue, strict rules. No drilling, limited loads, regulated access: we design free-standing structures.
  • Fashion: the stand is a shop window. Rails, shelves, mannequins and light on fabrics.
  • Two seasons a year. January and June: own the structure, change the graphics, store it with us.
  • Congresses and sponsors. Desks, backdrops and sponsor stands for congresses.

Building a stand at the Fortezza da Basso

  • A historic venue. The Fortezza da Basso is a 16th-century fortress in the city centre, a short walk from Santa Maria Novella station.
  • Access in the city. Trucks enter a busy historic area with traffic restrictions, so deliveries follow the organiser's time slots and we keep vehicles small where needed.
  • Pavilions of different sizes. Halls and pavilions inside the fortress have different heights and floor types: we check the exact pavilion before designing the stand.
  • Hotels. During Pitti Uomo the centre is full; book early or stay near the station to walk to the Fortezza.

How to choose a stand builder: 5 checks before you sign.

  1. Real photos of built stands: not just renders: ask for stands similar to yours in size and sector.
  2. A verifiable company: registered address and VAT number in Europe, and a phone someone answers.
  3. Who installs the stand: own crews or fixed partners, with one site manager on the floor until opening.
  4. Safety documents: Class 1 fire-rated materials and an electrical compliance certificate, as the fair rules require.
  5. An itemised quote: design, structure, graphics, furniture, power, transport, build-up, dismantling, paperwork and storage.

Who are the best exhibition stand builders in Italy and Europe?

There is no official ranking: the best one fits your fair, stand size and budget. Compare at least three builders on real photos of built stands, a verifiable company and VAT number, who actually installs the stand, fire-rated materials and an itemised quote.
