What TTG Travel Experience is and what it asks of a stand

TTG Travel Experience is Italy's main B2B travel trade fair: Italian regions and tourism boards, foreign destinations, tour operators, agencies, hotel chains, transport and travel technology. It takes place every year in October at Rimini Expo Centre, together with the fairs dedicated to hospitality and to hotel and beach furnishings.

It is a fair of meetings: agents and buyers arrive with a full agenda and move from table to table. The stand has to welcome people, make the destination immediately recognisable and have enough stations to work. For a foreign destination it is the most direct way to reach the Italian travel trade.

What a stand for TTG Travel Experience needs

  • Appointment tables. Stations with the company name, well distributed, for many meetings at once.
  • Image of the territory. Large-format photos, LED walls and local materials that tell the place before you read its name.
  • Joint pavilions. Common area and identical stations for co-exhibitors, with centralised graphics and build.
  • Welcome and tastings. A counter for coffee and local products that invites people to stop.
  • Event space. A small stage or a screen area for presentations, press conferences and workshops.

What your stand builder does at TTG Travel Experience

For TTG Travel Experience we handle design and 3D renders, production, graphics, electrics, transport, build and dismantling at Rimini Expo Centre, or just the part you are missing, even only the build of a stand you already own. In particular:

  • Stands for TTG Travel Experience.
  • Joint pavilions for regions and destinations.
  • Stations for B2B appointments.
  • Build at Rimini Expo Centre.

When to start preparing your TTG Travel Experience stand

TTG is in mid-October: design and graphics are prepared in spring–summer. For joint pavilions it is better to start earlier, when participations are collected. Rimini is close to our bases and warehouses, so site visits and build are easy to organise.
