Floating stage at the Venice Arsenale for The Heart in Art.

A light show on the water, in the basin of the Arsenale, for a French client. We turned the floating platforms into one stage: a level deck, carpet throughout and glossy black sides that reflect the lights.

Get a quote for a similar set-upThe set-up, photo by photo

In short. On 19 and 20 November 2026 Danova Stand fitted out the floating stage for The Heart in Art, a light show in the basin of the Venice Arsenale, for a French client. On floating platforms already moored and connected, we levelled the triangular steel joint between the modules with plywood panels, laid carpet on every platform and clad the sides in glossy black panels chosen to reflect the show lighting. Two days on site, partly in the rain, and a very satisfied client.

Why glossy black

The whole show was about light. A raw or pale side would have broken the scene; glossy black disappears in the dark and gives back the reflections of the moving heads and LED bars, adding to those on the water.

Before the finish, the deck had to be solved: the floating platforms were joined at an angle by a triangular steel frame. We closed it with made-to-measure plywood at the same height as the modules, so performers walk on one surface with no steps or gaps, and the carpet runs from end to end.

Two days on site, rain included

In Venice you work with what the city gives you: materials delivered by boat, tight spaces and a schedule set by the show. It rained during the set-up and we still handed over the finished stage on time.

On the water the sequence matters: deck first, then carpet, then side panels, then hand-over to the lighting crew. Each step has to be finished before the next one starts.
